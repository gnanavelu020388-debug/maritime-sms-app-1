How to see the demo

  1. Click "Open All Four at Once" above (or click each card one by one)
  2. If a pop-up is blocked, allow pop-ups or click each card individually — each opens in its own tab
  3. Arrange the three windows side by side on your screen
  4. Super Admin shows the platform overview with tenant ledger — click "Login As" on any company to instantly open that tenant's Company Admin workspace
  5. Company Admin shows fleet operations: vessels, crew directory, user management, and billing for the selected tenant
  6. DPA shows the safety & compliance dashboard: SMS document approvals, CAPA items, audit review, and vessel safety oversight
  7. Vessel Portal shows the read-only SMS document tree — use the header dropdowns to switch vessels and crew members
  8. Click Sign Out in any window to open the Demo Session Switcher — switch to any tenant, any role, or exit the demo
